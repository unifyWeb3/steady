# Demo Editing Handoff (for video editor / Remotion agent)

Source: raw screen recording + narration per `demo.md`. Output: one 2–3 min MP4, 1280×800 minimum, no generic AI-video styling.

## Pacing & cuts
- Hard cuts between the 10 shots in `demo.md`; no transitions longer than 200ms. Total ≤ 3:00.
- Silence trimming: cut wallet-popup waiting dead air to a 1s "signing…" title card, then cut back on hash appearance.
- Keep all real waiting that proves liveness (countdown tick, receipt poll) — cut only dead air.

## Captions & callouts (facts, not hype)
- Lower third on first terminal shot: "Steady · DreamDEX Event Contracts · Somnia Shannon testnet".
- Hash callouts (mono, copyable in description): overlay each tx hash for ≥3s: execution, redemption `0x3aa5ec…`.
- Policy callouts: zoom 110% on the Policy Control Gate box at 1:10; caption "Policy decides before the wallet signs".
- Actual-vs-quoted emphasis: split overlay "quoted 0.049 → filled 0.021" held 3s. This is the technical centerpiece — do not rush it.
- Section titles (5, serif, 1s each): Problem / Decision / Policy / Execution / Proof / Discipline / Why it matters.

## Polish rules
- Subtle zooms only (≤110%) on ticket numbers, policy badge, receipt hashes.
- Final CTA frame: production URL + "Testnet only · Not financial advice" for 4s.
- No stock footage, no AI voiceover (use the recorded human narration), no gradient backgrounds, no emoji, no sound effects except a single soft click on execution.
- Export: MP4 H.264 + SRT captions; also upload unlisted YouTube link for the BUIDL form.
