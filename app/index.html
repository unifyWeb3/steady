Without Steady

Price looks like a number. Max loss is hidden. Two losses → revenge trade. Winnings sit unclaimed because Finalized is hidden from the live list. Settlement is a black box.

With Steady

Max loss is the first number. Policy blocks bad trades with an exact reason. Fills are traceable (quoted vs actual). Settlement is a Finalized scan + ERC-6909, void is 0.5. Redemption is one click with explorer evidence.
