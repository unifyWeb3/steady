The problem
Short windows reward speed.
Your account needs friction.
Without Steady

Price looks like a number. Max loss is hidden. Two losses turn into a revenge trade. Winnings sit unclaimed because Finalized markets vanish from the live list. Settlement is a black box.

With Steady

Max loss is the first number. Policy blocks bad trades with an exact code. Fills show quoted vs actual. Settlement is a Finalized scan plus ERC-6909 balances — void pays 0.5. Redemption is one click with explorer evidence.

Control loop
Decide → Check → Execute → Verify.

Four steps, one accountable trail. The market moves fast. Your rules shouldn't.

01 — Decide
Honest ticket
Max loss → quantity, pay, payout. Tick/lot snapped. Spread and expiry visible before you sign.
02 — Check
Policy gate
Trading status 1, headroom ≥ 60s, liquidity, spread, balance, 2-loss cooldown. Denies with a code, not a shrug.
03 — Execute
IOC with proof
Immediate-or-Cancel (type 2). Expiry now + 120s capped at market expiry − 10s. Fixed 60 gwei / 10M gas.
04 — Verify
Receipt + fill
Tx hash, receipt, fill price vs quoted, order and fill IDs, explorer link, position lifecycle.
