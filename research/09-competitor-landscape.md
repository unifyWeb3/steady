# 09 — Competitor Landscape (Framework)

For each relevant competitor we record target user, problem, workflow, model, diff, weakness, moat.

| Product | User | Problem | Workflow | Model | Diff | Weakness | Moat |
|---------|------|---------|----------|-------|------|----------|------|
| Kalshi | US retail | Need legal prediction | Yes/No 0.01-0.99, $1 payout | Fee per trade | CFTC, clean UX | Thin niche liquidity, limited markets | Regulation |
| Polymarket | Crypto native | Deep prediction | Order book, USDC | Fee | Liquidity, variety | Offshore, complex for normies | Liquidity |
| DreamDEX app | Somnia traders | On-chain perps/spot/events | CLOB, zero fees, tUSDC | No fee | Fully on-chain, CEX speed | Event UI thin, no risk/scoring | Tech |
| TradingView | Chartists | TA | Charts, alerts | Sub | Charting | No on-chain execution | Brand |

We outperform on risk/education UX for short-window binaries, not on liquidity depth or market variety (can't).
