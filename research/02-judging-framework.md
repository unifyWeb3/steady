# 02 — Judging Framework

**Source:** SRC-001; rubric weights verified on DoraHacks detail.

| Criterion | Weight | Key judge questions |
|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| Innovation & Originality | 20% | Novel? Creative use of EC? Real problem? More than thin wrapper? Different interaction? |
| Technical Implementation | 25% | EC integration meaningful? Load-bearing? Robust lifecycle? Realtime/on-chain used correctly? Real execution not fake? |
| UX & Design | 20% | Understandable immediately? Better than DreamDEX itself for chosen use case? Value visible? First-timer comprehensible? |
| Business & Ecosystem Impact | 20% | Attract new users? Increase volume/frequency? Expand ecosystem? Sustainable model? Survive beyond hackathon? |
| Presentation & Demo | 15% | Problem in seconds? Visually memorable? 2–3 min workflow? Before/after? Real transactions? Survives skeptic? |

## Interpretation
- Technical is highest weight — need correct handling of 13 gotchas, real txs, no fake demo.
- Innovation rewards differentiated wedge, not "add AI".
- UX rewards plain language over crypto jargon, especially for Up/Down 15m windows.
- Business rewards retention + repeat, not just one-off volume.
- Presentation rewards live tx hashes + expiry-aware demo that doesn't break if market rolls mid-demo.

We will produce dual scores: opportunity score (12-factor) + judging score (5-factor) in 17-scorecard.
